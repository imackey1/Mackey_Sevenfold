Overview

Sevenfold is a two-player card battler decided by dice. You choose one of three champions, and your champion decides which cards you may use. Each champion owns a pool of 14 cards, for 42 cards in the whole game. You build a deck of 7 from your pool, then take turns clashing with an opponent.

Every card shows dice notation such as 2d6, meaning roll two six-sided dice and add them up. Each card also has a second, different effect called a post-mortem.

Turn flow

  1. Play a cardBoth players choose one card from their remaining deck and play it face up.
  2. Post-mortems triggerEvery card already in a graveyard triggers its effect, oldest first. Each one shakes and glows with a text label, before any dice are rolled.
  3. Roll the diceBoth players roll the dice on their played card, plus any dice added by post-mortems, and apply bonuses and penalties.
  4. ClashThe higher total wins. The loser loses one life. A tie means nobody loses a life.
  5. GraveyardThe card you played moves to your graveyard and starts working next turn.

Lives

Each player starts with 5 lives and the maximum is 6. You lose the game when you reach 0. Life gain is rare: only 3 of the 42 cards can restore a life.

If both players play all 7 cards and nobody is out of lives, the player with more lives wins. A tie is settled by a final 1d20 roll-off.

The graveyard

A post-mortem is the second effect on a card, and it often does no damage at all. Examples: add a die to your roll, re-roll your lowest die, lower the opponent's roll, ignore your next lost clash, or gain a life.

Post-mortems keep triggering every later turn while the card is in the graveyard. Effects marked Once fire a single time and then dim as Spent. See it happen on the Strategy Table.

Champion strategy

▲ Phoenix

Aggressive. Play high dice early and let post-mortems pile bonus dice onto every later roll.

  • Cards like Cinder Strike add +1d4 to every roll once they reach the graveyard.
  • Ashen Wing lowers the opponent's roll by 2 each turn, which adds up over a match.
  • Rising Ash is rare: save it for when you are behind on lives.

◆ Gambler

Risky. Re-rolls and gambles can swing a clash either way, so play them when you need a big result.

  • Loaded Dice re-rolls your lowest die every turn it sits in the graveyard.
  • Wager rolls a 1d6 and adds +1d6 to your roll on a 5 or 6.
  • Lucky Coin is rare and only pays off on a 6, so do not rely on it.

● Tide Warden

Defensive. Shield lost clashes, weaken opposing rolls, and outlast the other player.

  • Tidal Wall lets you ignore your next lost clash, once.
  • Undertow lowers the opponent's roll by 1 every turn.
  • Mend is rare and restores one life, up to the maximum of 6.

Glossary

Post-mortem
The effect a card has after it is played. It triggers from the graveyard on every later turn, before the dice are rolled.
Clash
The comparison of both players' dice totals. The higher total wins and the loser loses one life.
Once
A post-mortem marked Once triggers a single time. The card then shows as Spent.
Rare
One of three cards, one per pool, that can restore a life. A rare card carries a RARE tag.
Roll-off
A final 1d20 roll each, used only when both players have played all seven cards with equal lives.
