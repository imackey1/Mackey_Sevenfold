How it works

Four steps from first click to first clash.

  1. Choose a champion

    Your champion decides which cards you can use. Open the Lobby.

  2. Build 7 of 14

    Pick seven cards from your champion's pool of fourteen. Go to the Deck Table.

  3. Roll the dice

    Graveyard cards trigger first, then both players roll. Play a Quick Duel.

  4. Learn the rules

    Lives, graveyards and dice notation, explained in two minutes. Read the rules.

Meet the champions

Hover, tap, or tab to a champion to flip the card. Each one has 14 cards that no other champion can use.
