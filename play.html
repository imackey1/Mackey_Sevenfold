Play a Quick Duel

Pick one card each round for three rounds and see how the clash plays out. Pre-computed demo This page has no scripts, so every possible path was played in advance with the game's real rules and saved into the page. Your choices change which cards trigger, what you draw and which dice you get, but the dice for each path are fixed, so the same choices always give the same result.

The duel

Quick Duel: round 1 of 3

  • Restart duel
  • Recorded battles
  • Rules

You are Phoenix. Your opponent is Rookie Bot (Tide Warden).

Both players start with 5 lives and a hand of 3 cards. Each round you pick one card to play. Rookie Bot has no plan: it always plays the leftmost card in its hand, which is Tidal Wall first. Cards you played earlier sit in your graveyard and trigger their post-mortems at the start of each later round, before the dice. After three rounds, the player with more lives wins.
