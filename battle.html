Battle Table

Watch three full recorded matches against Rookie Bot, one for each champion. Recorded demo They were played offline with the same rules as the game, then saved into the page. Nothing here is rolled live, so each match replays the same way every time.

How the bot decides

Rookie Bot has no planning at all: it always plays the leftmost card in its hand. Because it draws one card after each turn to the right-hand end of its hand, it plays its deck in the order it was built. In these recordings, you play whichever card in your hand has the highest average roll.

A live bot that chooses while you watch would need JavaScript, which Project 1 does not allow. The recorded matches show the same rule: graveyard post-mortems, then dice, then the clash, then each player draws one card.
